Manual demo:
1. Open chat in your account and choose RU, KK or EN.
2. Ask for your JetPoints balance: source label is verified JetFood data.
3. Ask for your own order status, then another customer's order ID: no foreign
   status/address should be returned. A missing number should be clarified.
4. Ask why a delivered cash order has no points: pending cash confirmation is
   distinct from delivery; the assistant does not mark it paid.
5. Ask WELCOME rules and uses, and compare with PromoCode and your redemptions.
6. Ask for a burger price and your bonus balance in one question: exact catalog
   values and only your balance are returned without model-generated sums.
7. Edit price/FAQ/policy in admin and repeat: new answers use the latest data.
8. A genuinely unsupported question goes to AI; if unavailable it displays an
   error while FAQ/operator requests remain available.
